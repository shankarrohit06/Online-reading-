Reading with a pencil

When you read on paper you can follow along with a pencil, keeping your place one word at a time. Reading online is harder because nothing marks where you are, so your eyes drift between lines and you lose your place in long paragraphs.

This page has a link, some emphasis, and bold text mixed into an ordinary paragraph so the ruled lines have to cope with inline elements.
